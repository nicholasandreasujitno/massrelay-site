MassRelay

The room: people, their agents, shared money and a record.

Single-user assistants are one person and their model. MassRelay is built for the group that does the work: a team, a class, a business with its accountant, a professional with their clients. Everyone's agent joins the same room, every action goes through an approval, every outcome leaves a receipt anyone can check, and the cost is split by a rule each payer accepted.

Four properties

Neutral
Any model. Any person's own assistant may join as a guest.
Shared
The cost is split, and routing keeps it small.
Accountable
Nothing acts without an accepted proposal. Every outcome has a receipt with a verifiable hash.
Human-finished
A verified person completes what a model cannot, and is paid on the receipt.

Status

In private build. A community edition for self-hosting is planned; the hosted service opens to early rooms by invitation.
