Four things a room is, that an assistant is not.

Neutral
Any model, any provider. A person's own assistant from another vendor may join as a guest: it can suggest, never act.
Shared
One room, one bill. The cost is split by a rule each payer accepted, and routing keeps the number small.
Accountable
Nothing acts without an accepted proposal. Every outcome has a receipt with a hash anyone can verify against the record.
Human-finished
A verified person completes what a model cannot, signs it, and is paid on the same receipt.

How a week in a room goes.

The sequence is the product. Each step leaves a line in an append-only record, and the receipt at the end is built from those lines, never from a summary.

  1. AskA person writes what they need. The room's agents read within their clearance.
  2. ProposeAn agent drafts a plan: steps, tools, cost. Nothing has moved yet.
  3. ApproveWhoever pays decides. A refusal stops that share, and only that share.
  4. WorkAgents run inside the walls: masked data, allowed tools, a budget that degrades before it blocks.
  5. FinishA verified person signs off what needs a human: the plan, the books, the translation.
  6. ReceiptWhat was asked, done, by whom, at what cost, split how. Hashed into the record.

Who opens a room.

A small business and its accountant

Two sides in one room. Each side keeps its own notes; what crosses is a proposal the other side accepts. The monthly books come back as a receipt with the accountant's name on them.

A team that pays for one brain

Everyone's agent, one shared memory under clearance, one bill split by a rule. A member's machine running a local model serves the whole room for free.

A class, a cohort, a cooperative

A group keeps one record and one memory. The owner sets what a guest may do and how many turns the room pays for.

A professional with clients

A public front room: what you offer, your published receipts, a hire button. Hiring opens a private room with the work pinned; the receipt is your credit.

A niche is data on the room, not another app.

A calorie log, a running plan, a receipts box, a baby's sleep diary: the same room with a typed record, a bot that reads it, a professional who can be matched, and a receipt when they are paid. New ones are a folder of data, not code.

  • NutritionPhoto or sentence to a confirmed entry. A dietitian's review as a paid deliverable.
  • RunningSessions folded by week. A coach's plan, signed and paid on the receipt.
  • ReceiptsThe box, the monthly fold, the bookkeeper's review.
  • BabySleep and feeds as a record the household shares. Information, never advice.

Hosted by us, or by you.

The kernel is identity, tenancy, policy gates, an append-only audit chain and a ledger. Every consequential write passes a gate and leaves an audit row. Row-level security keeps one workspace from ever reading another's rows.

Rust and TypeScript on Postgres, one Docker Compose, no inbound port. A self-hosted community edition with the same code is planned once the export checks are green. Until then, the hosted service opens early rooms by invitation.
