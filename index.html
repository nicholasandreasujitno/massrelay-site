What it solves.

Six things people actually come with. Each shows where the agent stops and the person starts, and what the free part is.

Lose weight without guessing

Apps guess your calories from a photo and move on. Nobody checks, nobody adjusts, and the plan you paid for was written for someone else.

Your agent turns a photo or a sentence into an entry and asks when it is not sure. It folds your week and answers over your own record. When you want a plan, it finds a registered dietitian who reads your actual weeks and signs one.

in early access Free on your own device · a dietitian's plan priced by the dietitian

Get the books done, signed by an accountant

The receipts are in a drawer, the software "auto-categorised" half of them wrong, and the person who signs your taxes has never seen the rest.

Your agent folds the month from the bank export and the receipts, shows you the plan before it writes anything, and hands the result to your accountant. The accountant checks, fixes and signs. You get one receipt that names both.

in early access Free to run · the accountant's review priced by the accountant

Find a tutor who finishes what the app cannot

Homework apps explain the same thing five ways and the child still does not get it. A parent needs a person, on the one topic, this week.

The child works with the agent; the agent notices what is not landing and, with the parent's yes, finds a tutor for exactly that. One session, one topic, one receipt. The record of what was learned stays with the family.

Meet people this week, not someday

New in a city, or just tired of eating alone. Apps match profiles; nobody actually sets a table.

"This week: dinner, a run, co-work." Your agent opens the window, finds people who opened the same one, within a distance band and only with their consent, and sets the time. You turn up. Nobody pays anybody.

Get it done today, by someone real

A translation that must be certified, a contract someone should actually read, a form a professional has to file. The AI draft is not the deliverable; the signature is.

Your agent takes the intake and prepares everything. A verified professional does the part only a person can do and signs it. You pay when it is verified, and the receipt is proof for both of you.
