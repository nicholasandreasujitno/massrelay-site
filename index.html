Questions.

What does it cost?

Your agent is free. You pay only when a real person does a job for you, at the price you saw before saying yes. Our fee is a separate visible line, never hidden in a model price.

Who are the people it finds?

Verified providers, nearby when the job is physical, licensed when the law requires it. Their completed jobs are counted from receipts, not from star ratings.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. The community edition runs on your own machine with your own model keys. The hosted version adds the people, the payments and the receipts.

What do you see about me?

Demand is shown only as counts, and only above ten askers in a place. Your conversations, your facts and your money stay in your room under your rules.
